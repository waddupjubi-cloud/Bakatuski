Directly radiating from her chest plate and her eyes is a blinding, brilliant starlight aura that pulses with pure white and golden hues. Extending from her back, substituting for a traditional cape, is a pair of massive, ethereal wings made of pure, translucent energy that shifts dynamically between deep magenta, royal purple, and cosmic violet.

These energy wings trail off into wisps of stardust, constantly shedding tiny, glowing geometric particles and small, cross-shaped stars that drift lazily into the atmosphere. Around her weapons, particularly her dual spear and hovering blades, the aura manifests as volatile, high-frequency energy arcs—electric blue lightning bolts snap violently around the blue spearhead, while a dense, shimmering heat distortion warp surrounds the golden-orange blade, signifying a character capable of manipulating both the frozen void of deep space and the intense heat of a birthing star.

Recommended Atmospheric Environment
To perfectly complement this character's design and narrative weight, she would look exceptional positioned within a grand, high-contrast cosmic sanctuary or an ancient, crystalline orbital palace. Imagine her floating effortlessly above a sprawling, hyper-futuristic stellar citadel built into the rings of a massive, glowing gas planet. The background should feature deep, velvety voids of space interspersed with swirling, vibrant nebulas of magenta, teal, and deep indigo, casting dramatic rim lighting across her porcelain skin and metallic armor.

To emphasize her dominion over space, massive cosmic structures—such as giant, revolving golden astrolabes, floating monoliths inscribed with glowing neon runes, and the distant, blinding curvature of a nearby star—should frame her silhouette. The ground beneath her should be a polished, reflective crystalline or obsidian floor that mirrors her floating blades and the radiant energy of her wings, creating a profound sense of scale, divinity, and untamed power that suits her stellar aesthetic perfectly.
